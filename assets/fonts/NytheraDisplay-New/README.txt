Nythera Display individual editable SVG glyphs

Source: NytheraDisplay-Regular(3).ttf.
A.svg is the previously approved enlarged-point variant, kept unchanged.
All other characters use their exact source font outline curves.
These SVGs use transparent backgrounds with white vector paths.
Coordinates are in font units and include advance-width spacing.
The vertical SVG canvas is enlarged to avoid clipping tall/descending glyphs (notably N).
Includes A-Z, 0-9 and all punctuation present in the source TTF, including a blank space glyph.

No bitmap tracing or image generator used.
